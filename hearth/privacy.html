Hearth Privacy Policy

Effective 23 August 2026 · Hearth is made by Creative Camel Studio, Bengaluru

The short version: everything you put into Hearth stays on your device. The app has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. We cannot see your tasks, and neither can anyone else.

What Hearth stores

Your tasks, tags, settings (such as your name and daily capacity) and your streak are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They never leave the device unless you choose to export them.

What Hearth does not do

  • It does not create accounts or ask you to sign in.
  • It does not send your data to us or to any third party.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporters, advertising SDKs or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not access your contacts, calendar, location, photos or microphone.
  • It does not require an internet connection.

Notifications

If you turn on "Two gentle nudges", Hearth schedules two local reminders a day (a morning plan and an evening close) using iOS's own notification system. These are generated on your phone; no notification server is involved. You can turn them off in the app's Review screen or in iOS Settings at any time.

Backups

"Export" creates a plain JSON file of your data and hands it to the iOS share sheet so you can save it to Files, AirDrop it, or send it wherever you like. Where it goes after that is entirely your choice. "Import" reads such a file back into the app.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. "Fresh start" inside the app lets go of your open tasks without deleting them; they remain on your device until you delete the app.

Children

Hearth collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of Hearth ever adds an optional feature that sends data off the device (for example, sync), this policy will be updated first and the feature will be off by default.
