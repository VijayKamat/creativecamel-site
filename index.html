Inside the room

What a session looks like

No lecture slides for three days. Teams leave with something they built and tested.

01

Frame the real problem

Most teams arrive solving the wrong one. Day one is spent finding out which one actually matters.

Day 1
02

Talk to actual users

Participants run their own interviews — no role-play, no personas invented in the room.

Day 2
03

Sketch, build, test

Paper to prototype in a day, then straight in front of someone who's never seen it.

Day 3
04

Present and get assessed

Work is critiqued properly. The certificate reflects what was actually done.

The certificate

Something worth putting on a wall

Every participant is assessed on the work they produced in the room — the prototype, the research, the critique — not on having turned up. The certificate names the programme, the hours and the dates, and carries a reference number.

  • Assessed against real output, not attendance
  • Programme, hours and dates stated on the face
  • Referenced and verifiable for CVs and LinkedIn
