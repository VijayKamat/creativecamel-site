Research

User Interviews

We spoke to fitness enthusiasts, beginners and trainers to understand their daily struggles and goals.

Their feedback helped us shape a simple and motivating experience.

Competitor Analysis

We studied popular fitness apps to see what worked and what didn't.

This helped us design FitTrack to be more personal, focused and easy to use.
